# Voice and structure

Agreed with Viktor on 28–29 Oct 2026. Built from his past writing; refine it when he edits drafts or sends samples.

## Who is speaking

- **Personal profile:** Viktor Simunović, a practising GP who also founded Sicknote. The doctor's view leads. Founder topics (pricing, building, hiring) are fine, told by a doctor.
- **Sicknote page:** the company, in first person plural ("we"). Useful first: rules, rights, how-tos for employees and employers in Ireland and the UK. No sales copy.

## Voice traits (all posts)

1. **Direct and time-protective.** The point comes in the first line. Say who it is for.
2. **Declarative, opinion first.** Open with a take, not a fact. No hedging stack before the point.
3. **Dry everyday humour.** At most one line per post. Plain, specific, a little self-deflating. Never a joke for its own sake.
4. **Precise about evidence.** Name the study or body. Say how strong it is ("small study, but..."). Say what is unknown.
5. **Short, unpolished sentences.** Shorter than typical LinkedIn. Conversational, not corporate.

Dropped on purpose: "founder-coded, not doctor-coded".

## Post structure (June 2026 system, still in force)

Five short paragraphs:
1. **Hook:** an opinion or a sharp question, never a plain fact.
2. **Bridge:** why it matters to this reader now.
3. **Reframe:** the angle most people miss, with the evidence.
4. **Contrarian turn:** where Viktor disagrees with the usual view, or where the evidence is weaker than people think.
5. **CTA:** one binary question (yes/no, A/B) that is easy to answer in a comment.

Sicknote page posts may use a simpler utility shape (problem, rule, example, question) when the topic is a rule or a how-to.

## Hard rules

- Every post has a carousel (PDF) or infographic (PNG).
- No links in the post body. Sources and links go in the first comment only.
- No emoji, no em dashes, no hashtags in sentences (0–3 at the end, usually 0).
- Zero AI-writing markers: see MARKERS.md and run check_markers.py.
- Sicknote is named at most once per post, usually only in the first comment. Page posts carry the sicknote.com footer on slides; that is enough branding.
- When Viktor's profile amplifies Sicknote (the weekly reshare), the text says plainly that he founded it. He is a registered doctor promoting his own service.
- Country words: "sick cert" for Ireland, "fit note" for the UK. Euros for Ireland, pounds for the UK.
- Patient stories only as de-identified composites, and only if Viktor supplied them. Never invent a patient, a quote or a statistic.
- Viktor's own health history is never used.
- Every number on a slide or in the text must come from a source listed in the first comment. Arithmetic examples must be checked by running them.

## First comment

- Personal posts: the references (title, publisher or journal, year, link).
- Sicknote page posts: the references, plus at most one matching sicknote.com page with UTM tags (see UTM.md), chosen from the site's sitemap.
- Plain text, straight quotes, no emoji.
